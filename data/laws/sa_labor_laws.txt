[SA-LABOR LAW ARTICLE 98] Standard Working Hours
An employee may not actually work for more than 8 hours a day if the employer adopts the daily standard, or more than 48 hours a week if he adopts the weekly standard.
During the month of Ramadan, the actual working hours for Muslims shall be reduced to a maximum of 6 hours a day or 36 hours a week.

[SA-LABOR LAW ARTICLE 104] Rest Days
Friday is the customary weekly rest day for all employees.
The employer may replace this day for some of his employees with any other day of the week, provided strict adherence to the 1-day-off-per-week rule.
The weekly rest day shall be fully paid and not less than 24 consecutive hours.

[SA-LABOR LAW ARTICLE 101] Break Periods
Working hours and rest periods shall be organized so that no employee works for more than 5 consecutive hours without a break for rest, prayer and meals of not less than 30 minutes.
The employee shall not remain at the workplace for more than 12 hours a day.

[SA-SAFETY REGULATION] Shift Turnaround
For aviation safety critical roles, a minimum rest period of 12 hours is required between shifts to mitigate fatigue in high-temperature environments.
