THE WORKING TIME REGULATIONS 1998 (UNITED KINGDOM)
PART II: RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

4. Maximum weekly working time
(1) Unless his employer has first obtained the worker’s agreement in writing to perform such work, a worker’s working time, including overtime, in any reference period which is applicable in his case shall not exceed an average of 48 hours for each seven days.

10. Daily rest
(1) An adult worker is entitled to a rest period of not less than eleven consecutive hours in each 24-hour period during which he works for his employer.

11. Weekly rest period
(1) An adult worker is entitled to an uninterrupted rest period of not less than 24 hours in each seven-day period during which he works for his employer.
(2) If his employer so determines, they may fix two uninterrupted rest periods each of not less than 24 hours in each 14-day period.

12. Rest breaks
(1) Where an adult worker’s daily working time is more than six hours, he is entitled to a rest break.
(3) The rest break is a period of at least 20 minutes which the worker is entitled to spend away from his workstation if he has one.
