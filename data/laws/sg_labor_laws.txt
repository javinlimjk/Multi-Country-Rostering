[SECTION 38] Hours of Work
An employee shall not be required under his contract of service to work more than 6 consecutive hours without a period of leisure.
An employee shall not be required to work more than 8 hours in one day or more than 44 hours in one week.
However, if the employee works 5 days or less a week, the limit is 9 hours in one day or 44 hours in one week.

[SECTION 38-8] Maximum Daily Working Hours
No employee shall under any circumstances work for more than 12 hours in any one day.
This includes overtime work. This limit applies to all shift workers unless a specific overtime exemption has been granted by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM).

[SECTION 38-5] Overtime Limit
An employee shall not be permitted to work overtime for more than 72 hours a month.
Overtime work refers to all work in excess of the normal hours of work (excluding breaks).
Work done on a Rest Day or Public Holiday is not counted in the 72-hour limit unless it exceeds the usual daily working hours.

[SECTION 40] Shift Workers
For employees engaged in shift work, the 44-hour weekly limit may be averaged over a continuous period of 3 weeks.
An employee engaged in shift work may work more than 8 hours a day, provided the average number of hours worked over any continuous period of 3 weeks does not exceed 44 hours per week.
No shift worker shall work more than 12 hours in any single day under any circumstances.

[SECTION 36] Rest Days
Every employee shall be allowed one whole rest day each week without pay.
A rest day comprises one whole day (midnight to midnight).
For a shift worker, the rest day can be a continuous period of 30 hours. A 30-hour rest period that starts before 6pm on a Sunday is considered as 1 rest day.
The employer shall determine the rest day.

[SECTION 37] Work on Rest Day
An employee cannot be compelled to work on a rest day unless under exceptional circumstances (e.g., accident, essential service, urgent work).
If an employee works on a rest day at the employer’s request, they must be paid:
1. One day's salary if they work up to half the normal daily working hours.
2. Two days' salary if they work more than half the normal daily working hours.

[SECTION 88] Interpretation of Week
"Week" means a continuous period of 7 days commencing at midnight on Sunday and ending at midnight on the next following Sunday.

[CAAS / SATS SAFETY POLICY] Minimum Rest Period
To comply with aviation fatigue management standards and ensuring operational safety, rostered staff are required to have a minimum continuous rest period of 10 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
This is an operational hard constraint to prevent fatigue-related accidents on the tarmac.
